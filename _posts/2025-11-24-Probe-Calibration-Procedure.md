# Protocol Procedure for Probe Calibration and Tris Cal 

  
## Probe Calibration 
- Frequency: Once per week 

### Materials
- Orion StarTM A325 pH/Conductivity Portable Multiparameter Meter (Probe Set 1)
- Digital Thermometer Cat 150778
- Conductivity Standard (KCl = 53.0 mS/cm at 25.0 degrees C)
- DI water
- Kimwipes

### Steps 
1. Turn on Orion Star A325 Probe Set 1. Navigate to the calibration page by selecting "Cal" in the bottom left corner of the screen by pressing f1. Next, select "Conductivity - Channel" using f2. 
2. Rinse the salinity probe and temperature probe with DI water. Dry them with a Kimwipe. 
3. Place both probes into the 50 ml falcon tube labeled "Salinity / Conductivity Standard". Select "Start" by pressing f3.  
4. Slowly warm the solution to 25 degrees C by gently holding or squeezing the tube with your hands. Carefully move the probes around to ensure uniform temperature. 
5. Allow the reading to stabilize when the solution reaches 25 degrees C.
6. If the meter does not read 50.00 mS/cm, select "Edit" using f3 and manually enter 50.00 as the standard value. 

### Cleaning and Storage
1. Gently wipe the probes with a Kimwipe and DI water to remove any salt that may have accumulated. 
2. Store the salinity probe in a falcon tube with DI water. 


## Tris Cal
- Frequency: Once per week

### Materials
- Orion StarTM A325 pH/Conductivity Portable Multiparameter Meter (Probe Set 1)
- Digital Therometer Cat 150778
- Tris Buffer (50mL falcon tube labeled "BT 3/4 Tris")
- DI water
- Kimwipes
- Putnam Lab Notebook
- KCl storage solution

### Steps 
1. Turn on Orion Star A325 Probe Set 1. Rinse the pH probe and temperature probe with DI water, then dry with a Kimwipe. 
2. Place both probes into the 50 ml falcon tube labeled "BT 3/4 Tris". 
3. Create a table in the Putnam Lab Notebook to record your data. Title one side "Temp (degrees C)" and the other side "pH (m/V)". 
4. Measure pH every 0.5 degrees C starting from 22 degrees C to 28 degrees C and record in notebook. Slowly warm the solution by gently holding or squeezing the tube with your hands. Carefully move the probes around to ensure uniform temperature. 
5. Update the measurements in Github under the Data folder titled "Tris_Calibration".  

### Cleaning and Storage
1. Gently wipe the probes with a Kimwipe and DI water to remove any salt that may have accumulated. 
2. Place the pH probe back into the KCl storage solution.
