# Protocol procedure for feeding corals and cleaning coral plugs and side walls
  
## Feeding
- Frequency: Once per week
- Duration: 10 minutes per feeding round
  
### Materials
- Coral crates (in system)
- 3 feeding Bins
- Coral Max (coral food)
- 5 cc scoop
- 2 cup measuring cup 

### Steps
1. Turn off the pumps to the system by flipping the labeled switches located behind the tank and remove one half of the tank lids. 
2. Place 3 feeding bins on top of the lids that remain on the tank. Fill each bin with 4 cups of tank water. Avoid spilling water into the middle crack of the system. 
3. Add 1 scoop (5 cc) of Coral Max into each bin and swirl until mixed. 
4. Carefully place coral crates into feeding bins. If space is limited, move corals into additional bins as needed. Ensure each coral is submerged. Add more water if neccesary. 
5. Turn the pumps of the system back on and allow corals to feed for 10 minutes.
6. After 10 minutes, turn the pumps off again. Remove the coral crates from the bins and return them back into the system. Replace any corals that were moved back into the appropriate crate. 
7. Swirl the remaining food in the feeding bin to resuspend it.
8. Repeat steps 4 through 6 for a second feeding round using the same food.
9. After 2 rounds of feeding, use the measuring cup and remove some water from the feeding bins. Pour down the floor drain. Pour the remaining water out of the feeding bins down the floor drain.
10. Repeat this process for all corals in Blue Tank 1/2 and Blue Tank 3/4. 

### Cleaning Materials and Put Away
1. Rinse the feeding bins and 2 cup measuring cup thoroughly with hot water at the sink. 
2. Place them on the drying table and allow them to dry for 24 hours. 
3. Put away Coral Max and 5CC scoop. If a new container of Coral Max was opened, label it with "Opened", the date and your initials. 
4. Replace the lids to the tank and turn the pumps back on. 

## Cleaning Coral Plugs
- Frequency: Once per week

### Materials
- Coral crates (in system)
- Replacement crates (same number as the one being cleaned)
- Toothbrush
- Bleach bucket
- Tweezers
- Putnam Lab Notebook
- Kim Wipes (if necessary)
- Bleach bucket

### Steps
1. Turn off the pumps to the system by flipping the labeled switches located behind the tank and remove one half of the tank lids. Place your tools in a reachable secure area.
2. Find the replacement crate in the clean crate bin, located near the curtain, for the crate in the tank you would like to clean. Place the replacement coral crate into the tank next to the crate you are cleaning. 
3. Remove the coral plug from the crate, holding it by the base or sides of the plug. Try to not touch the coral itself. 
![Holding Coral Plug](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jessica-mongeau/Mongeau_Putnam_Lab_Notebook/master/images/HoldingCoralPlug.jpg
)
4. Remove algae on plugs by gently brushing over it with a toothbrush. Take the toothbrush and brush along the top of the plug where the coral and plug meet to remove any algae. Brush along the sides, base and bottom of the plug to ensure all algae has been removed.
![Cleaning Coral Plug](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jessica-mongeau/Mongeau_Putnam_Lab_Notebook/master/images/CleaningCoralPlug.jpg
)
7. In cases where filamentous algae is found on coral plugs that cannot be removed with toothbrush, remove it by gently plucking it off with tweezers. 
8. In cases where organisms, such as polycheate worms, are found on the coral plugs, remove them with tweezers and dispose with a Kim wipe. 
9. Place cleaned plug into replacement crate.
10. Repeat steps 5 through 9 for the remainder of the plugs. 
11. Remove the empty, dirty crate from the system and submerge it completely into the bleach bucket located in the sink for 24 hours.
12. Report which crate was cleaned into the Putnam Lab Notebook and Coral Plug Cleaning Log located on the refrigerator.
13. Turn the pumps back on after every 3 crates cleaned for at least 10 minutes. 

### Cleaning Materials and Put Away
1. When cleaning is complete, place the toothbrush in the bleach bucket for disinfection for 24 hours.
2. Rinse the tweezers thoroughly with hot water in the sink. 
3. Return the lid to the tank and turn the pumps back of the system back on. 

## Side Wall Cleaning 
- Frequency: Once per week
- Note: Best to be done when feeding corals as less crates will be in the tank, making it easier to clean.

### Materials
- Sponge
- Toothbrush

### Steps
1. Turn off the pumps to the system by flipping the labeled switches located behind the tank and remove one half of the tank lids. Place your tools in a reachable secure area.
2. Using the sponge, gently clean the sidewalls in a back and forth motion or in circular motions. Avoid splashing water into the middle crack of the system.
3. Use the sponge to gently clean the filters. 
4. Use a toothbrush to gently clean probe holder and probes.

### Cleaning Materials and Put Away
1. When cleaning is complete, place the toothbrush and sponge in the bleach bucket for disinfection for 24 hours.
2. Return the lid to the tank and turn the pumps back of the system back on.
