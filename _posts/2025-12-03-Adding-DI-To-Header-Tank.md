# Protocol Procedure for Adding DI Water to Header Tank
  
## Steps
1. Turn the blue knob labeled #5 (located at the bottom of the DI Tank), parallel to the pipes to allow water to flow to the vertical center pipe.
2. Turn both red knobs on the pipes leading from the vertical pipe towards the Header Tank parallel to allow water to flow.
3. Switch on the "Water and DI Water" control on the panel to the left of the DI Tank. The indicator light will turn on when activated.
4. Allow a small quantity of DI water to flow into the Header Tank.

## Shut Off Procedure
1. Switch off the "Water and DI Water" control on the panel. 
2. Turn the red knob closest to the header tank perpendicular to the pipes to inhibit water flow. Do the same for the rest of the knobs. 
3. Mix water in the Header Tank with your hand. 
4. Check the salinity after 1 hour.
