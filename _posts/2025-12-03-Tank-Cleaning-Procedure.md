# Protocol Procedure for Tank Cleaning
  
## Side Wall Cleaning 
  - Frequency: Once per week
- Note: This task is most efficient when performed during coral feeding, as fewer crates will be in the tank, making surfaces easier to access. 

### Materials
- Sponge
- Toothbrush

### Steps
1. Turn off the pumps to the system by flipping the labeled switches located behind the tank and remove one half of the tank lids. Place your tools in a reachable secure area.
2. Using a sponge, gently clean the sidewalls in a back and forth motion or in circular motions to remove accumulated algae. Avoid splashing water into the middle crack of the system.
3. Gently clean the filters with a sponge or toothbrush to remove any algae that has accumulated that may prevent water flow. 
4. Using a toothbrush, carefully clean the probe holder and probes to remove algae.

### Cleaning and Storage
1. Once cleaning is complete, submerge the toothbrush and sponge in the bleach bucket and allow them to soak for disinfection for 24 hours.
2. Return the lid to the tank and turn the pumps back of the system back on.
